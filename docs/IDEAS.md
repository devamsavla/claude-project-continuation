# 50 Mindy ideas

The rule behind every idea: Mindy notices, asks, or does — the person never has to know a feature exists.
Each idea uses data Mindy already has or gets by asking one tap question, and costs almost nothing to run.
**Now** = in the current build. **Next** = small, planned. **Later** = needs partners, money or an app.

## Make it theirs
1. **Name your Mindy** — name, colour and personality (Sweet, Cheeky, Calm). **Now**
2. **The pet question** — "Did you ever have a pet?" → "Call me Bruno? 🐶". **Now**
3. **Brain Score (0–1000)** — fills as you fill your mind, keep streaks, do saved reels, catch scams. Shareable card. **Now**
4. **Mindy skins** — party hat, chef hat, cricket cap, unlocked at score milestones. **Next**
5. **Badges** — Memory Keeper, Scam Buster, Birthday Hero, Streak Master. **Next**
6. **Mindy Wrapped** — year in review: places saved, habits kept, scams caught, people remembered. Made to share. **Next**

## Mindy asks (each person is their own database)

8. **Your Mind in 3D** — a brain whose regions light up as you fill them. **Now**
9. **Family tree** — add people as you go; birthdays, sizes and gift ideas hang off each person. **Now**
10. **Goals with a Sunday check-in** — "how did it go? 1–5". **Now**
11. **Habit streaks** — reply *done*, streak kept. **Now**
12. **"What do you know about me?"** — see, edit, delete every fact. **Now**
