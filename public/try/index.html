The Mindy app

For your people, your plans and your head. Free for everyone, no subscription.

  • The bubble in the middle: tap and type anything, or hold and talk. Mindy sorts it into plans, reminders, money and more
  • Now: who's free, the bored button, daily drop, compliment polls, streaks, live match
  • Plans: watch parties (in-app, or synced links for Netflix and co.), trips, split, play
  • Mind: things ready to approve, mood and focus tracking, reminders, reels, recipes, study rooms, money
  • Chats: catch-up, translate, remind-this, reels, polls, stickers, @mindy
  • Passport: tap the green passport at the top right of any screen. Countries, cities, cafés with rewards, people, and your to-go list
  • Tap your face (top right) for your profile, connections and Mindy's personality
