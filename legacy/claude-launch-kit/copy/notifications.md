# Mindy's notifications

Three personalities the user picks in the app (Me → Mindy's personality). Rules for every one:
max 2 a day, never between 22:00 and 08:00 local time, always about something the user saved, planned or asked for.

## Sweet 🥹
- Arjun's birthday is tomorrow. I already picked a card. You just press send. 🎂
- You saved 3 recipes this week. Sunday + paneer tikka = a love story.
- Mum's evening in Mumbai starts in 30 min. Perfect time for a call. 📞
- 48 photos from Zoya's party are waiting. You look great in 11 of them.
- You kept 17 of 18 reminders this week. I'm genuinely proud. 🥹

## Cheeky 😏
- Rent's due in 4 days. I'm not saying you forgot. I'm saying I didn't.
- India need 47 off 30. Your heart rate and my notifications, both rising. 🏏
- It's Friday. You eat out 6 Fridays out of 8. Shall I just book the usual?
- You saved that vada pav reel 3 times. At this point it's a relationship.
- Psst. Priya just made a plan and you're in it. Don't leave her on read.
- Your gym reminder is ringing. Your sofa is also ringing. Choose wisely.
- Haven't heard from you in 2 days. Did you find another second brain? 👀

## Savage 💅
- Third pasta reel this week and zero pasta cooked. I'm keeping score.
- You snoozed 'call mum' twice. She raised you. Call her.
- Arjun is #1 on the leaderboard. Again. Just letting you sit with that.
- Your passport expires in 40 days. Lisbon won't visit you.
- Hand cricket record: 2–7 against me. I'll go easy. (I won't.)

## On WhatsApp
Inside 24 hours of the user's last message these can go as normal messages. Outside it, WhatsApp needs an approved template, and re-engagement copy counts as Marketing (opt-in required, charged per message), so use them sparingly there and keep the playful ones for app push notifications.
